How the radar scores a protocol

Every number below is computed at run time from the sealed snapshot itself. No protocol name is ever special-cased, and nothing is typed in by hand.

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier
  • Category multiple — the median market-cap / TVL of the live-token protocols in the same category. A category with fewer than 3 live samples falls back to the global median. Clamped to 0.05 – 1.50.
  • Growth multiplier — 1 + clamp(7d change, −60%, +120%) ÷ 100 × 0.5. A protocol with no reported 7d change counts as 0%.
  • Maturity multiplier — 0.6 + min(age in days, 1095) ÷ 1095 × 0.4, so a protocol tracked for 3 years scores 1.00 and a brand-new one 0.60. Unknown age counts as 365 days.

How to read the board

Rank 1 is the largest implied value. A WATCH badge marks the snapshot's 40-protocol watchlist (TVL $50M+, records no token, not a wrapped, staked or bridged product). The board also ranks 158 further pre-token protocols the watchlist leaves out — 138 below the $50M floor and 20 above it. A further 91 pre-token protocols are left out entirely: the board applies the watchlist's own rule to every row, so bridged, staked, wrapped, versioned and exchange-branded products are excluded wherever they sit. Click any row for its model breakdown and its six farm steps.

Disclaimer

Implied value is this dashboard's own claim, derived from the snapshot's figures; it is not a price, a prediction, or a promise that a token will ever be issued, or when. The snapshot is DefiLlama's record as fetched on the snapshot date, not proof: a protocol listed as recording no token may still have a token its record does not carry. One limit of the exclusion above: each snapshot record carries ten fields and no parent-protocol field, so a product of another protocol whose own record does not name it as one cannot be excluded automatically — the rule can only catch what the record shows. Farm steps are generic actions derived from a protocol's category, main chain, TVL and age; they are not financial advice. Never pay to claim an airdrop, and only ever follow links the protocol itself publishes.
